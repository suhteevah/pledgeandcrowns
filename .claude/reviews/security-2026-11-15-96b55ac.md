## Findings

- **P2-1 [inf], unproven toolchain dependency:** `prealloc` and
  `compiler_alloc` pin `allocations: 1`. std can allocate on its own (for
  example the stdout buffer on first print); whether that falls inside the
  counted window depends on std internals, which vary by rustc version. If
  the deploy toolchain differs from the dev box, those two missions could
  fail correct solutions. Correctness risk, not security. *(Orchestrator's
  note: the server refuses to build with anything but its pinned rustc
  1.99.0, see `compile-api/src/toolchain.rs`; the risk is therefore at the
  next toolchain bump, when these two missions must be re-proven.)*
- **P2-2 [code], wording:** `data.rs:1435` says the counts "come from the
  real sandbox run"; they were proven on Windows only (wasmtime, no bwrap).
- **P2-3 [code], carried over:** run-child stderr captured at 2 MiB
  (`run_sandbox.rs:309`) versus the 64 KiB the comment implies.
- The fifth review's P2-1 is closed (the trigger list fired this review); its
  P2-2 does not recur.
